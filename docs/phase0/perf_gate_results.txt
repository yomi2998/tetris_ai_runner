## Candidate contract

`src/candidate_format.h` normalizes both sides to physical placements:

- A candidate key is the sha-free FNV-1a hash of the four occupied cells sorted
  by index, computed from each side's own piece geometry table, so duplicate I,
  S, and Z orientation representations collapse to one candidate.
- Arrival history is dropped for every non-T piece.
- T keeps at most two candidates per physical placement: normal and terminal
  rotation.
- All 7 pieces times 37 corpus boards are reported, one row per case, plus a
  corpus hash over the whole ordered table. Empty-board normalized counts are
  T 60, Z 17, S 17, J 34, L 34, O 9, I 17, on both sides.

Timing notes: the timed unit on both sides is board preparation plus enumeration
plus contract normalization for one board, so shared contract work dilutes the
ratio toward 1. The `search_only` rows subtract a measured extraction
calibration per piece and per side; they are the stricter reading and also pass.
The machine runs the `powersave` governor with boost enabled and other
processes present, so absolute nanoseconds drift between runs; that is what the
ABBA interleaving within each repetition controls. Ratios are summarized as
medians of per-repetition pairwise ratios, not ratios of aggregate medians.
