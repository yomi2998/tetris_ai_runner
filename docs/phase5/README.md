# Phase 5 evidence

Value-based TOJ policy (`src/toj_policy.h`, `src/toj_policy.cpp`) with
lossless fixture parity against the frozen legacy policy (`src/ai_zzz.h`,
`src/ai_zzz.cpp`, unchanged during Phase 5).

## Staging

The legacy policy stays frozen and production harnesses keep using it
until the Phase 7 cutover. The new policy lives alongside it under
distinct symbols (`toj_policy::Policy`) so both can coexist in one
process for comparison. No compatibility bridge or forwarding overload
exists between them. Legacy deletion happens in Phase 8.

## Corpus

`fixtures/toj_policy_v2.csv` holds one self-contained case per line,
generated by the `migration_fixtures policy_v2` mode from the legacy
engine. Each case records source and result rows, piece and candidate,
witness flags, effective spin input, clear count, complete parent
policy state, complete decision context, lossless expected evaluation,
complete expected resulting state, the legacy safety value, and the
engine safety configuration. Doubles use 16-digit hexadecimal bit
patterns, so parity is bit-exact where the builds agree. The column
contract lives in `fixture_schema.md`.

Boards cover the 24 seeded stacks plus empty, tall, line-clear banks
(`o1`, `o2`, `t1`, `iwell3`, `iwell4`), T-slot boards (`slotS`,
`slotM`) for T3 descriptors and full T-spins, wedged pockets
(`mini0`, `mini1`, `mini2`) for T-spin minis, wedged doubles
(`double0` through `double3`) for full T-spin doubles at several
safety variants, and single-row banks (`pci` through `pci4`) for
perfect clears with live bonuses. Parent combo, B2B, under-attack,
map-rise, accumulation, hold, next-sequence, and engine safety
variants cycle deterministically across cases.

## Provenance

Generator command, byte determinism proof, cross-compiler comparison,
legacy commit pin, parameter hash, and combo-table hash are recorded
in `fixture_hashes.txt`.

## Gate status

`tests/toj_policy_tests.cpp` (CTest `toj_policy_tests`, 64,731
checks, 0 failures in all five builds) gates the corpus, the directed
contracts, and two synthetic formula cases.

Parity result: all 7,167 shared cases compare new outputs directly
against the frozen expected fields. Peak frozen drift is 0 ULP under
GCC self-release, at most 1 ULP under Clang self-release, and at
most 5 ULP under debug and sanitizer builds, against the 5 ULP
allowance; every non-floating field matches exactly
in every configuration. The 5 debug-only cases beyond oracle
precision (proven FMA contraction effects, integers exact) use a
same-build legacy comparison at the same bound, with frozen drift
reported, never gated. The corpus-side lockout split is 7,167 shared
with 0 divergent cases; approved lockout-semantic differences are
covered instead by directed `is_lockout` tests over every piece and
rotation on floor boards plus the rule-layer orientation corpus,
which pins lowest-row lockout against the legacy bounding row where
they agree.

Implementation notes: evaluation converts the result board to local
rows once and derives side columns on demand; perfect-clear detection
uses full-board emptiness while evaluation stays inside the 40-row
horizon; danger masks come from compile-time spawn geometry verified
per piece against legacy spawn rows; the transition takes the rule
outcome spin directly with no reclassification plus the caller-held
evaluation, so board evaluation stays once per unique board; the
lockout threshold uses the lowest occupied mino row. The transition
signature omits the plan's source-board input because neither legacy
`eval` nor `get` reads it; the result board is the sole board input,
exactly like the legacy code paths.

Sensitivity: the scaffold failed 6,033 checks, a danger-mask shift
failed 5,144, zeroed hold, waste, and slot terms failed around a
thousand each, a removed perfect-clear bonus failed its live cases,
an attack-scoring change failed dozens, and a restored non-spin
triple waste term failed exactly the synthetic directed case. A
heap-garbage `ai_config()->safe` read found during validation is
pinned per case in both the test engine and the fixture generator;
regeneration from the pinned generator reproduces the committed
corpus byte for byte.
